📦 Physical Clues: Fragment A

The Minotaur System has hidden Fragment A — a physical card containing the 6-character exit password — somewhere on campus.

ARI has triangulated its location. Follow the steps below to retrieve it. This is a real physical task — you'll need to go to the location and find the hidden envelope.

🗺️ Your Mission

  1. 1
    Go to the Location

    Head to Studio Shelf 7B. Look for a small hidden envelope with a red "NE" seal.

  2. 2
    Open the Envelope

    Inside you'll find a Fragment Card. It contains the 6-character exit password and a lore note.

  3. 3
    Photograph It

    Take a photo of the card for your records — this is part of your evidence collection.

  4. 4
    Memorize the Code

    The 6 characters on the card are your exit password. You'll enter them at the Exit Terminal.

  5. 5
    Return & Continue

    Come back here and go to the Exit Terminal from the Hub. Enter the password to escape.

⚠️

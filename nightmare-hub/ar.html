📍 QR on campus AR — How it works:
  1. Find the QR code left by ARI in Building J Floor 3 Stairway B window on campus (the designated AR target image).
  2. Tap “Poster AR Scan” Camera tool will open below!
  3. Point your phone camera at the QR to tap the link that takes you to the compromised digital space
  4. Once the sceurity loading screen has passed, aim your phone toward the ground.
  5. When you see "Place" tap it while you are pointing your camera downward.
  6. Once placed. Move yourself and the phone a bit back before tapping the gold ring.
  7. From there, it is up to you to help secure the envrionment by finding the correct hint, be careful and dont be locked in, exit back to hub if necessary.

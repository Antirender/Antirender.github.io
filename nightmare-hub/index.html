Trafalgar
Labyrinth

A cybernetic labyrinth has locked down the Sheridan Trafalgar campus. Navigate the maze, solve puzzles, collect fragments — and find the exit code before the Minotaur System finds you.

⏱ Estimated play time: 15–30 minutes
↓ Scroll to begin ↓
1

You Are a Participant

The Minotaur System has infiltrated the campus network. You've been recruited to shut it down.

Your guide is ARI, a distributed guidance system. ARI offers directions — but the Minotaur can mimic its signal. Trust wisely.

⚡ Core tension: Is ARI helping you, or herding you? One wrong turn and the labyrinth closes.
2

Solve Puzzles, Collect Fragments

The labyrinth contains 3 puzzles — each with 4 choices. Pick correctly to advance. Pick wrong and it's a dead end — you'll have to start over.

Along the way, collect evidence from physical clues, AR scans, and lore archives.

⚠️ No second chances: Each puzzle is single-choice. Choose carefully — wrong answers end your run.
3

The Hub Is Your Home Base

After entering your code, the Hub becomes your command center. It shows progress, unlocked nodes, and the next step.

Navigate between puzzles, physical clues, AR experiences, and more.

🧭 Route: Entry → Puzzle 1 → Puzzle 2 → Puzzle 3 → Exit Terminal
Puzzle 1 also unlocks Physical & AR exploration nodes.
4

Physical + Digital Touchpoints

The labyrinth extends into the real campus:

📦 Physical: Find hidden envelopes and fragment cards.

📷 AR: Scan QR codes and AR markers.

🗺️ Map: A live map shows node locations on campus.

🥽 VR: Optional immersive maze-room experience.

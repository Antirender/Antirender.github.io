Solar + Storage for AI Compute为 AI 算力供电的光伏储能电站

To give our AI compute enough power, I built a 49 kWp solar array and a 180 kWh battery bank on the rented roof of a farmhouse in Liuhe, Nanjing. The servers for AI pretraining and inference come first; the surplus goes to the farm and the village. My parents and I designed, wired and automated it ourselves — all self-taught.

Context背景

Our servers for AI pretraining and inference run around the clock, and without solar the site needs about 32,888 kWh a month. To give the compute enough power, I built a solar station on the rented rooftop of a farmhouse in Liuhe, Nanjing.

Because the roof belongs to a farming family, the station was planned to serve them and the village too, not just the machine room. There was no installer — my parents and I taught ourselves and built it. The brief:

  • AI compute first. Loads run on solar first, then the batteries; the grid is used only when both are empty.
  • Share the surplus locally. The farmhouse first, then the community microgrid — nothing is sold to the grid.
  • Keep control alive. A fault in the power stage must not take down the system that coordinates it.
  • Stay inside every limit — MPPT voltage and power, BMS current, cable and busbar ratings.
  • Grow in stages without starting over.

Constraints: a budget of about ¥120,000, 40 kg modules on rural rooftops, and summer heat — 46–49 °C outdoors, 38–42 °C in the equipment room, 57–60 °C on the transformers.

AI 预训练和推理的服务器 24 小时不停，不用光伏的话，全站每月要用大约 32,888 度电。为了给算力提供足够的电能，我在南京六合租下一户农民的房屋屋顶，自己建了这座太阳能电站。

屋顶是农户的，所以电站从一开始就不只为机房服务，也要让农家和村里受益。没有找施工队，我和父母边学边做。给自己定的要求：

  • 算力优先。负载先用光伏，再用电池，两者都没电时才切到市电。
  • 余电就地共享。先给农家，再进社区微网，不向电网卖电。
  • 控制系统不能跟着倒。功率部分出故障时，负责协调的控制系统要保持在线。
  • 每个器件都留在额定范围内：MPPT 电压和功率、BMS 电流、线缆和铜排的载流。
  • 能分阶段扩建，不用推倒重来。

限制条件：预算约 12 万元；每块组件约 40 kg，要装到农村屋顶上；夏季高温，室外 46–49°C，机房 38–42°C，变压器 57–60°C。

System系统结构

Power flows from the arrays through six inverter-chargers to the loads in priority order — AI compute, then the farmhouse, then the community microgrid — with the battery bank on a shared DC bus. The grid is wired in only as a backup input.

Sharing the surplus余电共享

The station is the green heart of the AI compute, but it also ties into the village. Once the servers are covered, the surplus stays local instead of going to the grid.

这座电站不仅是 AI 算力的“绿色心脏”，也是乡村振兴的“减碳纽带”。算力用电满足之后，余电留在村里，不送进电网。

Electric farm tools电动农具

Surplus goes to the farming family first. Electric farm tools charge whenever needed, so tilling, irrigation and transport no longer run on diesel — each kWh replaces fuel and cuts emissions in the field.

余电优先反哺农家：电动农具随时充电，旋耕、灌溉、运输告别柴油轰鸣，每一度绿电都替代原来的燃油，直接削减田间碳排放。

Engineering decisions工程决策

01Clipping on the left array → re-stringing左阵列削峰 → 重新分串

Problem问题
The left array ran as 5 strings of 9 modules. At noon a 9-module string makes about 6.3 kW.左阵列原来是 5 组、每组 9 块串联，正午一串约 6.3 kW。
Cause原因
One MPPT input handles at most 5.5 kW, so anything above that was clipped off.单路 MPPT 最大 5.5 kW，超出的部分被削掉。
Fix做法
In May 2026 the 45 modules were re-wired into 5 strings of 7 plus 2 strings of 5, with one more inverter taking the extra inputs. A 7-module string peaks near 4.9 kW, and its open-circuit voltage (≈336 V) stays well under the 500 V window.2026 年 5 月把 45 块改成 5 组 7 串 + 2 组 5 串，多分出一台机来接。7 块一串峰值约 4.9 kW，开路电压约 336V，都在上限以内。
Result结果
Every input now sits below its power limit. The shutdown cost about 380 kWh that month.每一路都低于功率上限。停机改串那个月少发了约 380 度。

02Fault isolation for the control system故障隔离：控制系统单独供电

Problem问题
The three parallel units are phase-locked by an Arduino + PLC. If the controller went down with a failing power unit, the whole output would follow.3 台并联机靠 Arduino + PLC 锁相位。如果控制器跟着故障的主机一起掉电，整个输出都会停。
Fix做法
One inverter powers only the control system and is fully isolated from the others. A sixth unit waits as a hot standby and switches in automatically when a main unit fails.单独用一台逆变器给控制系统供电，和其他机器完全隔离；再留一台热备，主机故障时自动切入。
Result结果
A fault in the power stage no longer takes the controller offline.主功率层出故障时，控制系统仍然在线。

03BMS “short circuit” on reconnect → pre-chargeBMS 重新接入报短路 → 预充电阻

Problem问题
After a BMS cut off and the bus lost power, plugging the charger back in made the BMS report a short circuit.BMS 断开、母线掉电之后，一接充电器 BMS 就报短路。
Cause原因
Inrush current into the DC-bus capacitors, which charge from 0 V almost instantly.母线电容从 0V 瞬间充电，涌流太大。
Fix做法
A pre-charge resistor brings the bus up gradually before the main connection closes.加预充电阻，先慢慢把母线电压充上来，再接通主回路。

Other issues along the way其他遇到的问题

  • Cables jumping at high current: battery cables moved on the floor and breakers tripped under heavy load. Positive and negative runs are now bundled together, and the breakers were replaced with DC fuses.
  • Outer-right string collapsing under load: 430 V open-circuit fell to 80 V at just 2 A. In May 2026 the series connections were re-made and the string split into its own group. The root cause is still being confirmed.
  • Heat: in summer it reaches 46–49 °C outdoors, 38–42 °C in the equipment room and 57–60 °C on the transformers.
  • 大电流时电缆跳动：重载时电池电缆在地上跳、断路器跳闸。现在正负线并在一起扎好，断路器换成了直流熔断器。
  • 外侧右组串带载塌压：开路 430V，带 2A 负载就掉到 80V。2026 年 5 月停机重接串联线，外侧右单独成一组。根本原因还在确认。
  • 散热：夏季室外 46–49°C，机房 38–42°C，变压器 57–60°C。

Reflection & next steps总结与下一步

Most of the problems came from a limit we hadn't designed around yet — input power on an MPPT, inrush into capacitors, the force between heavy cables at high current. Reading every rating before wiring anything became the habit that mattered most.

Separating control from power and keeping a hot standby made the system something we can leave running. Planning the busbar and inverter roles with room to spare let storage grow from 80 to 180 kWh without starting over.

  • Find the root cause of the outer-right voltage collapse.
  • Bring transformer temperatures down before next summer.

大多数问题都来自当时还没考虑到的某个上限：MPPT 的输入功率、电容的涌流、大电流时电缆之间的作用力。后来养成的最重要的习惯，是接线之前先把每个器件的额定参数看一遍。

控制和功率分开供电、再留一台热备，系统才能放心长期运行。铜排和逆变器的分工一开始就留了余量，所以储能从 80 kWh 扩到 180 kWh 时不用推倒重来。

  • 查清外侧右组串塌压的根本原因。
  • 明年夏天之前把变压器温度降下来。
